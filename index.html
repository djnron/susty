    Settling it

    Offsetting this costs about a hundredth of a penny.

    At roughly £90 per tonne for verified carbon removal, this conversation is a rounding error. Buying a credit for it is fine, but it isn't the useful move.

    These three take minutes and cover it thousands of times over:

    1. Wash at 30°C — around 200 g saved per load.
    2. Thermostat down 1°C — roughly 300 kg a year in a typical heated home.
    3. One beef meal a week swapped — on the order of 200 kg a year.
