What to do about it

Offsetting this costs under a hundredth of a cent.

At roughly $100 per tonne for verified carbon removal, this conversation is a rounding error. These three actions each cover it thousands of times over:

  1. Wash clothes in cold water, around 200 g saved per load.
  2. Thermostat down 2°F, roughly 300 kg a year in a typical heated home.
  3. One beef meal a week swapped, on the order of 200 kg a year.

    Notes

    We do our best to make these calculations as accurate as possible. Some of it has to be estimated, so here's where the numbers come from and where we're guessing. The full method has all of it.

    One thing we don't know precisely, but would be great to find out: which specific servers are answering your questions, in a data center somewhere else, running on their own electricity. We've assumed they're close to you and on the same energy sources, which is an estimate. We'd love to know exactly which servers are doing the work and what that data center draws, but that isn't public information right now. Our goal is to run this locally on a sustainable server ourselves.

    The sum itself is simple: energy used × how dirty the electricity is. That includes the screen you're reading on, so the total creeps up while the page sits open, not just when you ask something. And longer chats cost more per answer, because we send the whole conversation back each time.

    Methodology

    • Energy per token (0.05 / 0.5 mWh) Luccioni et al. 2023 — Power Hungry Processing: Watts Driving the Cost of AI Deployment
    • Data centre overhead PUE ×1.12 Uptime Institute 2023 Global Data Center Survey — global average power usage effectiveness
    • Embodied hardware carbon +15% Patterson et al. 2021 — Carbon Considerations for Large Language Model Development
    • Great Britain — live, by region NESO Carbon Intensity API. Half-hourly actuals for 14 distribution regions, so GB readings here are current rather than averaged. Open and keyless; nothing about you is sent with the request.
    • United States — hourly by region EIA-930 hourly generation by fuel type, turned into an intensity with EIA's own per-fuel figures (coal 2,257 lb/MWh, gas 976 lb/MWh). Two caveats worth knowing: EIA publishes hourly CO₂ only in bulk spreadsheets, so this is derived from the generation mix rather than read off, and the feed itself runs about a day behind. We therefore take the same hour on the previous day, which tracks the daily curve far better than their newest figure would — that one is the middle of last night. Interregional imports are ignored. Needs a free EIA key; without one we fall back to the annual figures below.
    • United States — annual, by eGRID subregion EPA eGRID2023 Rev 2 CO₂e output rates, converted from lb/MWh. Subregions run from 110 g/kWh in upstate New York to 680 on Oahu, so the 384 national average describes almost nobody. Used when no live reading is available.
    • Everywhere else — national annual averages 2024 country figures, cross-checked against Ember's Global Electricity Review 2025. Annual means, so they miss both time-of-day swings and regional spread.
    • Carbon removal cost ~$100/tonne Frontier 2024 State of Carbon Dioxide Removal — durable CDR market pricing
    • Dark mode display power (3–9% at normal brightness) Dash & Hu, MobiSys 2021 — How much battery does dark mode save? An accurate OLED display power profiler for modern smartphones. Measured across several OLED phones: ~42% of total phone power at 100% brightness, but only 3–9% at the 30–50% brightness in normal use.
    • Display power — modelled, not measured The browser can't tell us what panel you have, so the figures above come from the picker and the study beside it, not from your device. The OLED phone delta is calibrated into that 3–9% band; laptop and monitor rows scale it by panel area and are extrapolation, since only phones were measured. LCD is modelled as zero because a backlight's draw is content-independent — local dimming and mini-LED are a real exception we don't try to quantify. Screen-on time is genuinely measured, and pauses when the tab isn't visible.
